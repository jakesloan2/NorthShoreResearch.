Dispatch

Orders placed before 2pm Monday to Friday are dispatched the same day. Orders after 2pm, or at weekends and bank holidays, are dispatched the next working day. You'll receive a tracking link by email when your parcel leaves us.

Packaging and storage

Products are shipped in recyclable cardboard with paper void-fill. Powders and capsules should be stored in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight, with the lid closed. Do not freeze.

Damaged or missing deliveries

If your parcel arrives damaged, email a photo of the packaging and product within 48 hours and we'll replace or refund it. If tracking shows delivered but you haven't received it, check with neighbours and any safe place, then contact us within 7 days and we'll investigate with the courier.

Where we deliver

United Kingdom only at present (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). Some Highlands, Islands and NI addresses may take an extra day.
