Quality & testing

What we check, what we test, and what we publish.

We only sell products we can trace from the manufacturer to your door, and we publish the evidence where we have it. This page explains what we check, what a batch certificate shows, and what we don't claim.

Supplier checks

Before a product goes on sale we ask the manufacturer for:

  • their food business registration and a current third-party food safety audit (for example BRCGS or an equivalent standard);
  • the product specification and label, which we check against UK food supplement and labelling rules;
  • the certificate of analysis for each batch we receive.

We keep these records for every batch we sell.

Batch identification

Every tub, pouch and box carries a batch number and best-before date. Your order confirmation records the batch numbers we sent you, so if there's ever a question about a product we can trace exactly which batch you have and where it came from.

Independent testing

Where a batch has been tested by an independent laboratory, we publish the certificate in our certificate library. Depending on the product, testing covers:

TestWhat it tells you
Content (assay)That the product contains the amount of the main ingredient stated on the label.
Heavy metalsLead, cadmium, mercury and arsenic are within the legal limits for food.
MicrobiologyThe batch is free from harmful bacteria such as Salmonella.
Particle size / physicalMixability and consistency, for powders.

If a product page doesn't show "Batch tested", we haven't published an independent certificate for it. We'd rather tell you that than imply testing that hasn't happened.

What a certificate shows

  • the product and the batch number it applies to;
  • the laboratory that did the testing and the date it reported;
  • each test, the method used, the specification and the result.

Match the batch number on your product to the one on the certificate.

Labels and claims

Our labels list every ingredient, the amount per serving and allergens in bold. We only use health claims that are authorised for use in Great Britain, with the conditions attached to them. Food supplements aren't medicines, and we don't claim they treat, prevent or cure anything.

Storage and delivery

Stock is kept in a clean, dry, temperature-controlled store away from direct sunlight, and dispatched in sealed packaging. If anything arrives damaged or with a broken seal, don't use it. Contact us and we'll replace it.

Report a problem

If you think something is wrong with a product, stop using it, keep the packaging and get in touch with the batch number. We investigate every report and will recall a batch if needed.
