FAQ

Delivery, products, returns and everything in between.

Ordering & delivery

How quickly will my order arrive?
Orders placed before 2pm Monday–Friday are dispatched the same day. Standard tracked delivery takes 2–3 working days; express is next working day. You'll get a tracking link by email.
Is delivery free?
Standard UK delivery is free on orders over £200. Below that it's £3.95. Express is £6.95.
Do you deliver outside the UK?
Not at the moment. We're UK-only while we get the basics right.
How do I use a discount code?
Enter it in the discount code box in your basket or at checkout. One code per order; codes can't be combined.

Products

Are your products tested?
Yes. Every product carries a batch number. Where a batch has been independently tested, its certificate is published in our certificate library. The batch number on your tub links to its certificate — email us the batch number if you'd like a copy.
Are your products suitable for vegetarians or vegans?
It varies by product — check the Key features section on each product page. Our creatine and electrolytes are vegan; whey isolate is vegetarian; fish oil is neither.
Where are your products made?
We only stock products from manufacturers who give us their food business registration, a current third-party food safety audit and a certificate of analysis for each batch.
Can I take these if I'm under 18 or pregnant?
Our pre-workout is not recommended for under-18s or pregnant or breastfeeding women due to its caffeine content. For all products, food supplements should not replace a varied balanced diet — if you have a medical condition or take medication, speak to your GP or pharmacist first.

Returns & support

What's your returns policy?
Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. For food safety reasons we can't accept opened consumables unless faulty. See our returns policy.
My order arrived damaged — what do I do?
Email us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll send a replacement or refund straight away.
How do I contact you?
Email, phone or the form on our contact page. We reply within one working day.
